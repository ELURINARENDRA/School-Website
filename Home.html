FOUR DECADES OF LEARNING

Rooted in our region.
Ready for the world.

First established in 1982 as ABC Grammar School, ABC was the region’s first English-medium school. Today, it continues to help students build a strong academic foundation, think independently, and grow with confidence.

On a five-acre, green campus in Piduguralla, our learners grow through CBSE academics, modern classroom technology, and the values of discipline, humility, and empathy.
