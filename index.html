About Us

ABC Grammar School, as it was christened at its inception, was established in 1982 as the very first English-medium school in this region. Over the last 40 years, it has grown into a renowned name in the field of education, and is now fondly referred to by parents, teachers, and alumni as "ABC". ABC School's expansive and lush-green campus environment, spread over 5 acres of land, allows students to flourish in an ambience that symbolises abundance, growth, and constant learning.
